Developer is not liable for their outages, policy changes, suspension, pricing, security incidents or discontinuation.

Client must protect credentials, control users, maintain lawful privacy practices and approve ongoing maintenance. Client will not provide regulated sensitive data unless a separate signed agreement expressly covers it.

For **30 days** after launch, Developer will correct reproducible defects that materially fail the signed SOW. This excludes Client or third-party changes, misuse, new requirements, provider changes, attacks, hosting failures and third-party services. Ongoing maintenance requires a separate plan.

## 11. Disclaimer and results
Except for the limited warranty, services are provided “as is” to the extent allowed by law. Developer does not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, sales, revenue, regulatory approval or any business result.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive or consequential damages, or lost profit, revenue, data, goodwill or opportunity. ## 13. Client indemnity
Client will defend and indemnify Developer against third-party claims arising from Client-provided materials, Client products or services, Client legal violations or Client misuse of deliverables, subject to prompt notice and reasonable cooperation.

## 14. Confidentiality
Each Party will reasonably protect the other’s nonpublic business and technical information and use it only for the project. This excludes information independently developed, publicly available without breach, lawfully known or lawfully received from another source.

## 15. Termination
Either Party may terminate an uncured material breach after ten days’ written notice. Developer may suspend immediately for nonpayment, unlawful requests, abuse or security risk. Payment, ownership, confidentiality, disclaimers, liability, indemnity, disputes and records survive.

## 16. Governing law and disputes
The Parties will first attempt good-faith resolution through written notice and a conference. Arizona law governs. Unless attorney-reviewed arbitration language is added, exclusive venue is in state or federal courts in Maricopa County, Arizona.

The Parties consent to electronic records and signatures. Typing a name, checking acceptance, and selecting “Sign Agreement” with intent to sign constitutes an electronic signature. The system may retain the frozen contract, version, cryptographic hash, signer identity, timestamp, IP address and user agent as evidence. Each Party may retain a copy.

Developer is an independent contractor. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without written consent, except with a merger or sale. Force majeure excuses reasonable delay. Written email notice is permitted. Unenforceable provisions are narrowed without invalidating the remainder. This Agreement, the SOW and signed change orders are the complete agreement and may be amended only in a signed writing.
